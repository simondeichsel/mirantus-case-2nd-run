# Interview 3 — Optician, owner of 2 independent stores, Bavaria

Synthetic. Notes from a 45-min visit in her store, in German, translated by the interviewer where
marked.

- Runs mira in both stores since 2024. Around 30 screenings a month across both.
- Why she offers it: "Die Leute kommen rein, und manche kaufen danach eine Brille. Und es
  unterscheidet uns von der Kette nebenan."
- On reminders: [translated] "The customers are mine. If someone writes to them, it's me. I don't
  want them getting e-mails from a company they've never heard of."
- Her staff call customers when glasses are ready for pickup. She says they could "theoretically"
  call screening customers after a year, "aber wer soll sich das merken?" She has a CRM from her
  cash-register vendor; doesn't know if it can do reminders.
- Staff turnover is high; the trained examiner in store 2 left in summer, screenings there dropped
  to almost zero for 6 weeks.
- Thinks customers don't want to be bothered: [translated] "Older customers find it pushy if you
  call them for something they didn't ask for."
- When asked about her own data: knows that "a few" customers came back, can't say how many.
- Wish: "A list. Who should come back this month. Then my people can call."
