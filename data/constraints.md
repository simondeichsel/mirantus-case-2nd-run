# Constraints

Simplified for this case. Treat them as fixed.

## Consent (GDPR)

- Customers may only receive reminders or marketing if they gave `marketing_consent` at booking.
- Without consent, the only messages allowed are those needed to deliver the service already
  bought (booking confirmation, appointment reminder, "your result is ready").
- Consent can be asked for again later, but only in a message the customer already receives
  legitimately, or in person.

## Medical advice

- mira is a non-medical screening. It is not a replacement for an eye examination by an
  ophthalmologist.
- Anything that sounds like a diagnosis or a treatment recommendation may only come from the
  evaluating ophthalmologist, in the report.
- Recommending a screening interval to a specific customer counts as a medical statement: it must
  come from the ophthalmologist's evaluation, not from marketing copy.
- No fear-based messaging ("you could go blind").

## The optician owns the relationship

- By contract, the customer is the optician's customer. Mirantus may contact customers only for
  the service itself, or on the optician's behalf and with the optician's name on it.
- Opticians can't be required to do extra work, but they can be offered tools.

## Capacity

- One squad for one quarter: 2 engineers, 1 designer, you.
- The report PDF and e-mails can be changed. The opticians' own systems (their CRMs, cash
  registers) can't be integrated this quarter.
